What it finds

  • PERSON names of people, including a surname or given name used alone later
  • LOCATION places named for themselves: country, province, district, city, ward, tole
  • ORGANIZATION offices, companies, schools, banks, hospitals, parties
  • DATE dates that are not dates of birth, in both calendars
  • ADDRESS where a person lives, owns land or receives deliveries, as one span
  • DOB dates of birth
  • PHONE personal and direct work phone numbers
  • EMAIL personal and named work e-mail addresses
  • URL web addresses
  • DIGITAL_SECRET client IP addresses, device identifiers, logins and social handles, passwords, keys, profile links
  • NATIONAL_ID citizenship numbers, Aadhaar, SSN, NINO, SIN and similar
  • TAX_ID Nepal PAN, India PAN, ITIN, UTR, TFN
  • PASSPORT passport numbers
  • DRIVER_LICENSE driving licence numbers
  • VOTER_ID voter and election identity numbers
  • BANK_ACCOUNT bank accounts, IBAN, DEMAT and BOID numbers
  • CREDIT_CARD debit and credit card numbers
  • PAYMENT_ID UPI handles, wallet identities, payment handles, personal crypto addresses
  • MEDICAL_ID patient, hospital record and health insurance numbers
  • EMPLOYEE_STUDENT_ID staff, provident fund, permit, university registration and symbol numbers
  • PROPERTY_UTILITY_ID plot, sheet, survey, ration card, electricity, water and fibre consumer numbers
  • VEHICLE_ID number plates of private vehicles, chassis and VIN
  • ID_NUMBER any other number that names one person
  • OFFICE_PHONE phone numbers of offices, helplines and public services
  • OFFICE_EMAIL shared and institutional mailboxes

It reads Devanagari, Nepali written in Latin letters, Hindi and English, with digits of either kind. What has a fixed form, such as a phone number or a number plate, is found by rules; names and the numbers that need their context are found by the model.

Use it

In Python, with no graphics card:

Python
# pip install git+https://github.com/fiste-ai/rakshya
from rakshya import Rakshya

pii = Rakshya()
pii.redact("मेरो नाम सीता थापा हो, फोन ९८४१२३४५६७।")
# मेरो नाम [PERSON_1] हो, फोन [PHONE_1]।

From a shell, rakshya "some text" does the same. In a web page, load rakshya.js as the README shows; this page is the example. Weights Apache-2.0, code MIT. It is a helper and not a guarantee: in Nepali news it misses about 2 in 100 characters of personal data, and more on text that is unlike news.
